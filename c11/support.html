Frequently Asked

Q: I bought Pro / Lifetime but content is still locked.
Tap "Restore Purchase" on the paywall. Make sure you are signed in with the Apple ID used for the purchase. Purchases are shared across devices with the same Apple ID.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?
Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → SwiftUI Bug Tracker. Cancel anytime; access continues until the current period ends.

Q: A workaround doesn't work on my iOS version.
Each entry lists the exact affected and fixed versions. Workarounds can change behavior across releases — check the version matrix on the bug detail page, and let us know via email.

Q: Can I suggest a bug to add?
Yes — email us the Apple Feedback number or a minimal reproduction. Community-verified entries are prioritized.
