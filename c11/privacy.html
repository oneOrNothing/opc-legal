Privacy Policy

SwiftUI Bug Tracker (the "App") is developed and operated by OPC-APPFACTORY. This policy explains what data the App handles. Short version: we collect nothing.

Data We Collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

  • No tracking — no identifiers, no analytics SDKs, no advertising.
  • No third-party services — the App contacts no servers at runtime.
  • Local storage only — your reading history and preferences stay on your device using standard iOS UserDefaults.
  • Push notifications (Pro feature) — used only to notify you when new bug entries are added. Notification tokens are handled by Apple's Push Notification service and are not associated with your identity by us.

Purchases

All purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email, payment details, or purchase history. Refer to Apple's Privacy Policy for details.

Children

The App is a developer reference tool and is not directed at children under 17.

Updates to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date.
